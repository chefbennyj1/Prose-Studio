# AI Vocabulary Cheat Sheet: Red Flags vs. Human Fixes

AI scanners do not search for logic; they search for **predictability**. Large Language Models (LLMs) are trained to pick the most likely next word, creating highly mathematical, balanced, and generic rhythm patterns. 

This guide breaks down the ultimate phrase list of typical AI-generated text, why scanners flag them, and exactly how to rewrite them to capture an authentic human voice.

---

## 1. The "Textbook Jargon" Red Flags
AI models default to formal, encyclopedic categories instead of visceral, real-world descriptions.

| The AI Phrase | Why It Gets Flagged | Human Alternative |

| **Oxidizing disinfectants** | Highly predictable, medical manual phrasing. | *Bleach, harsh peroxide, scorched-earth spray* |

## 2. Transition and Structural Filler
AI text structure is notoriously rigid. It loves to signal its direction before moving forward, which creates artificial "burstiness" patterns.

*   **"In conclusion..." / "To sum up..."**
    *   *AI Usage:* Used at the end of almost every generated text block to neatly package thoughts.
    *   *Human Fix:* Drop the transition entirely. Start directly with your closing thought.
*   **"Let's dive in..." / "Delving deeper..."**
    *   *AI Usage:* Used as an introductory transition to shift focus.
    *   *Human Fix:* *"Let's look at,"* *"Here is how,"* or simply dive straight into the data point.
*   **"It is important to note that..." / "Crucial to remember..."**
    *   *AI Usage:* Verbal clearing of the throat used to pad out sentence length.
    *   *Human Fix:* Delete the phrase. If the point is important, the data should show it.
*   **"Furthermore..." / "Moreover..." / "In addition to..."**
    *   *AI Usage:* Used mechanically to stack paragraphs or bullet points evenly.
    *   *Human Fix:* *"Also,"* *"Plus,"* or start a fresh, blunt sentence.

## 3. The "Dramatic Narrative" Clichés
When writing fiction, essays, or summaries, AI defaults to an exaggerated, melodramatic tone that relies on predictable pairings of adjectives and nouns.

*   **"A stark reminder..."** (AI uses this constantly to create a fake sense of emotional gravity).
*   **"Navigating the complexities..."** (Instead of a character just doing something difficult).
*   **"Standing as a beacon of hope..."** (A highly predictable metaphorical pairing).
*   **"Not just X, but Y..."** (A specific syntactic structure AI uses to balance a sentence).
*   **"Shed light on..."** (The go-to phrase for explaining an investigation or discovery).
*   **"At the end of the day..."** (A conversational cliché that AI overuses to mimic casual speech).
*   **"Rich history / Deep-rooted tradition..."** (Generic boilerplate used for any historical description).

## 4. The Structural Tell: Sentence "Burstiness"
Scanners look closely at **Burstiness** (variation in sentence length and structure). 
*   **AI Writing:** Follows a rhythmic, steady pattern. Long sentence, medium sentence, long sentence. Grammatically flawless, perfectly balanced, and totally flat.
*   **Human Writing:** Jagged. It uses fragments. It uses short bursts. Like this. Then, a human writer might string together a beautifully complex, rambling thought that builds up speed before crashing down to a swift stop. 

### Contrast Example:
*   **AI Style:** *The dark hallway smelled strongly of oxidizing disinfectants and recycled air, creating a sterile environment that made the patient feel incredibly anxious.* (Balanced, predictable, passive).
*   **Human Style:** *The air in the hallway was thin, stale, and recycled. It tasted like bleach. She gagged.* (Unpredictable, jagged, active).
